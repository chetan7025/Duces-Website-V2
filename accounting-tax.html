UAE VAT INFORMATION

Understanding VAT Registration Thresholds

UAE VAT registration depends on the value of taxable supplies, imports and, where relevant, taxable expenses.

MANDATORY REGISTRATION

AED 375,000

The mandatory threshold for UAE-resident businesses is generally based on taxable supplies and imports over the previous 12 months, or expected in the next 30 days.

VOLUNTARY REGISTRATION

AED 187,500

Eligible businesses may apply voluntarily when the applicable taxable supplies, imports or taxable expenses exceed the voluntary threshold.

Important:

Registration requirements depend on the business's actual activities and circumstances. Special rules may apply, including to non-resident businesses. The thresholds above are general guidance, not an individual eligibility decision.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Accounting & Tax FAQs

When does a UAE business need to register for VAT?

For UAE-resident businesses, mandatory registration generally applies when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 over the previous 12 months or are expected to exceed that amount in the next 30 days. Eligible businesses may qualify for voluntary registration above AED 187,500.

Is Corporate Tax registration required for Free Zone companies?

Free Zone status does not automatically remove Corporate Tax registration obligations. Taxable persons, including Free Zone Persons, generally need to register, subject to the applicable legislation and exceptions.

How often must VAT returns be filed?

VAT return filing frequency is determined by the Federal Tax Authority. Businesses should check their assigned tax period and the deadline shown in their EmaraTax account.

What is the Corporate Tax return filing deadline?

Corporate Tax returns and any tax payable are generally due within nine months from the end of the relevant tax period. Specific circumstances should be checked against current FTA guidance.

What documents are generally needed for accounting services?

Depending on the service, documents may include trade licence details, invoices, receipts, bank statements, sales and purchase records, expense records and previous tax filings.

Can a new company use bookkeeping services?

Yes. New businesses can organise their financial records from the beginning, helping maintain clearer transaction records and prepare for future reporting requirements.

Does DUCES guarantee tax registration or approval?

No. Tax registration, filing acceptance and other decisions are subject to the applicable laws, documentation and Federal Tax Authority procedures. DUCES does not guarantee government approvals or specific tax outcomes.
