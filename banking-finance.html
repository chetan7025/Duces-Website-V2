Business Banking

Business Bank Account Assistance

A business bank account is an important part of operating a company in the UAE. The appropriate banking option depends on the company's legal structure, licensed activities, ownership, business model, expected transactions and documentation.

DUCES helps business owners understand available banking options, review general requirements and prepare for the application process.

Discuss Your Business Account

Common Documentation

  • Valid UAE trade licence
  • MOA or company formation documents, where applicable
  • Shareholder and authorised signatory identification
  • Passport, visa and Emirates ID, where applicable
  • Office lease or Ejari, if required
  • Business profile and activity description
  • Expected turnover and transaction details
  • Invoices, contracts or supporting business evidence
  • Source of funds and ownership information

Final requirements are determined by the selected bank.

Business Finance

Business Loans & Finance Assistance

Business finance may help eligible companies manage working capital, business expansion, equipment purchases or other approved commercial requirements.

DUCES helps business owners understand general finance options, review preliminary requirements and organise supporting documents before approaching a suitable lender.

Finance eligibility can depend on company turnover, profitability, bank statements, business history, existing liabilities, ownership and the lender's credit assessment.

What May Be Reviewed?

  • Company operating history
  • Annual turnover and financial performance
  • Business bank statements
  • VAT returns and financial records, where applicable
  • Existing loans and financial commitments
  • Business purpose and requested finance amount
  • Shareholder or guarantor information, if required

Property Finance

Mortgage Loan Assistance in the UAE

Whether you are considering purchasing a home or exploring property finance, understanding the available mortgage options and documentation is an important first step.

DUCES provides general guidance on mortgage requirements and helps clients organise information for discussions with relevant lenders.

Mortgage terms and eligibility vary according to the applicant's income, residency status, credit profile, property value, down payment and lender policies.

Common Mortgage Considerations

  • Applicant income and employment or business profile
  • Residency and identification documents
  • Existing financial commitments
  • Property value and type
  • Available down payment
  • Credit history and lender assessment
  • Property and sale documentation

Frequently Asked Questions

Banking & Finance – Common Questions

Is DUCES an official partner of the banks displayed?

No. DUCES Consultancy Services operates independently. The bank names displayed are for general information and comparison. DUCES does not claim an official partnership or representation.

Can DUCES help a newly established company?

Yes. We can explain general banking requirements and help organise documents for a newly established company. Account opening remains subject to the bank's policies and due diligence.

Does DUCES guarantee business account opening?

No. Account opening decisions are made independently by banks following their internal compliance and approval procedures.

Can DUCES guarantee a business loan or mortgage approval?

No. Finance approval, amount, interest or profit rate, tenure and other terms are determined by the relevant lender after assessment.

Do all UAE banks have the same requirements?

No. Documentation, eligibility, account conditions and service charges can vary between banks and may change over time.

How can I get started?

Contact DUCES with a brief description of your requirement. We can discuss the information needed for an initial review.
