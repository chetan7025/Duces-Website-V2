Frequently Asked Questions

UAE Banking & Finance – Common Questions

Important information for companies and individuals exploring banking and finance options in the UAE.

1. Can a newly established UAE company open a business bank account?

Yes, newly established companies may apply. However, each bank has its own eligibility criteria, due diligence procedures and document requirements. Account opening is subject to bank approval.

2. What documents are generally required for a business bank account?

Common documents include a valid Trade Licence, MOA or company formation documents, shareholder and authorised signatory identification, office or lease details and information about the business activity. Additional documents may be requested by the bank.

3. Can non-resident shareholders apply for a UAE business bank account?

Some banks may consider companies with non-resident shareholders. Requirements vary by institution, ownership structure, nationality, business activity and risk assessment. The bank determines eligibility.

4. Do all UAE banks have the same minimum balance requirements?

No. Minimum balance requirements, account fees, transaction limits and other charges differ between banks and account packages. Always confirm the latest terms directly with the bank.

5. How long does business bank account opening take?

Processing time depends on the bank, company profile, ownership structure, completeness of documents and due diligence checks. There is no guaranteed opening timeline.

6. Can a Free Zone company open an account with a UAE bank?

Free Zone companies may apply to UAE banks, subject to the bank's policies and acceptance criteria. The company activity, jurisdiction, shareholders and supporting documents may be considered.

7. Can an established UAE company apply for a business loan?

Established businesses may explore financing options. Banks generally assess turnover, account conduct, financial statements, company history, existing liabilities and repayment capacity.

8. What is generally considered for a business loan application?

Common considerations include company financial performance, bank statements, business activity, revenue, outstanding facilities, credit profile and supporting financial documents. Requirements vary between lenders.

9. Can a UAE resident apply for a mortgage?

Residents may apply for mortgage finance if they meet the lender's eligibility criteria. Income, employment or business profile, existing liabilities, property details and down payment may be assessed.

10. Does DUCES guarantee bank account opening or loan approval?

No. DUCES provides independent consultancy, document preparation guidance and application coordination. All account opening, financing and mortgage decisions are made solely by the relevant financial institution.

11. Can DUCES assist with POS machines and online payment gateways?

DUCES can help businesses understand general requirements and explore available payment acceptance solutions. Availability, pricing, onboarding and approval depend on the respective provider.

12. Does DUCES work as an official representative of the banks shown?

No. DUCES is an independent consultancy. Bank names and logos are included for general identification only. No official partnership, agency or endorsement is implied.
