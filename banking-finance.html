Business Banking

Business Bank Account Assistance

A suitable business account supports your daily operations, collections, supplier payments and financial management.

Choose with a Clear Understanding

Selecting a bank involves more than comparing account fees. Business activity, ownership structure, expected transactions, customer geography and source of funds can all influence a bank's assessment.

DUCES helps you understand the available options, organise documentation and prepare for the bank's application and verification process.

Typical Documents to Prepare

  • Valid UAE Trade Licence
  • Memorandum of Association, where applicable
  • Shareholder and authorised signatory documents
  • Passport, visa and Emirates ID copies, as applicable
  • Company address and tenancy documents, where required
  • Business activity and operating model details
  • Expected transaction and source-of-funds information
  • Invoices, contracts or supporting business records, if requested
Business Finance

Business Loan & Financing Guidance

Explore financing options for business requirements, working capital and planned expansion.

Understand Your Business Finance Profile

Lenders may consider the company's operating history, turnover, account conduct, profitability, existing liabilities and financial records when reviewing a financing application.

DUCES can help organise your business information and understand the documentation generally requested before you approach a lender.

Information Commonly Reviewed

  • Trade Licence and company ownership details
  • Business bank statements
  • VAT returns and financial statements, where applicable
  • Annual turnover and business performance
  • Existing loans and financial commitments
  • Business purpose and proposed use of funds
  • Invoices, contracts and supporting records

Frequently Asked Questions

Banking & Finance – FAQs

Does DUCES represent or partner with the banks listed?

No. DUCES is an independent consultancy. The bank names are provided for general information. We do not claim to be an appointed agent, official representative or partner of these institutions.

Can DUCES help me choose a business bank account?

We can discuss your business requirements, explain general account considerations and help you identify banks you may wish to approach. The bank independently decides whether to accept an application.

Is a business bank account guaranteed after applying?

No. Every bank conducts its own due diligence, KYC checks and eligibility assessment. Account opening is subject to the bank's approval and policies.

Can a newly established company apply for a business loan?

Requirements differ between lenders and financing products. Operating history, turnover, financial performance and other factors may be considered. We can help you understand the general requirements before approaching a lender.

Does DUCES guarantee loan or mortgage approval?

No. All financing decisions, rates, terms and approvals are made solely by the relevant bank or finance provider.

Can individuals and companies contact DUCES?

Yes. You can contact us to discuss business banking, business finance or mortgage-related guidance and documentation assistance.

Important: DUCES Consultancy Services provides consultancy, information, documentation guidance and coordination support.
