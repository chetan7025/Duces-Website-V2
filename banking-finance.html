Frequently Asked Questions

Banking & Finance FAQs

What documents are generally required for a UAE business bank account?

Requirements vary by bank and business profile. Commonly requested documents may include a valid trade licence, MOA or incorporation documents, lease or Ejari, shareholder and authorised signatory identification, and business activity or transaction information.

Can a newly established UAE company apply for a business bank account?

A newly established company may enquire with banks, but acceptance depends on the bank's policies, business activity, ownership, risk assessment and supporting documentation.

Does DUCES guarantee business account opening or finance approval?

No. DUCES provides independent consultancy and application assistance. Final approval, account opening, financing terms and limits are determined solely by the relevant financial institution.

Can DUCES assist with POS and payment gateway enquiries?

Yes. We can help businesses understand available POS, SoftPOS and payment gateway options and the general documentation involved. Provider approval and commercial terms remain subject to the provider's assessment.

Can an existing UAE business enquire about working capital?

Existing businesses can enquire about finance options. Lenders may review turnover, bank statements, financial records, business history, liabilities and other eligibility criteria before considering an application.
