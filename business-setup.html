FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Business Setup Questions.

What is the difference between Mainland and Free Zone?

Mainland and Free Zone companies operate under different licensing and regulatory frameworks. The suitable option depends on your activity, business model, customers and operational requirements.

Can a foreign investor own a UAE company?

Many UAE business activities allow full foreign ownership. Specific conditions depend on the activity, jurisdiction and applicable regulations.

How much does UAE company formation cost?

Costs depend on the authority, licence activity, office requirements, visa allocation and additional approvals. We recommend reviewing a complete cost breakdown before selecting a package.

Can I open a business bank account after company formation?

You can apply for a business bank account after formation. Banks conduct their own due diligence and approval assessment. A trade licence does not guarantee account approval.

Can DUCES help with visas and post-licence requirements?

We can coordinate relevant PRO, visa and related business support requirements, subject to the applicable authority process.

Important: Licence costs, visa eligibility, office requirements, approvals and processing times vary by jurisdiction and authority. All applications remain subject to the relevant authority's rules and final approval. DUCES does not guarantee licensing or approval outcomes.
