OPTION 03

Offshore Company Formation

Offshore structures may be considered for certain international holding, investment, ownership or asset structuring purposes. They are different from a Mainland or Free Zone operating company.

An Offshore company is not automatically suitable for conducting ordinary business operations inside the UAE. Local trading, office, visa and banking requirements must be examined separately.

DUCES can help you understand the proposed purpose, review jurisdiction options and coordinate enquiries with the relevant registered service providers.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Business Setup Questions.

Which is better: Mainland or Free Zone?

Neither is automatically better. The right choice depends on your business activity, customers, operating model, office requirements and future plans.

Can a foreign investor own 100% of a UAE company?

Many UAE activities permit full foreign ownership. Conditions depend on the activity, legal structure, jurisdiction and applicable regulations.

Can a Free Zone company do business in Mainland UAE?

This depends on the activity and applicable rules. Additional licences, permits, branches or other arrangements may be required for certain Mainland activities.

Can an Offshore company provide UAE residence visas?

An Offshore company generally does not provide UAE residence visa allocation in the same way as a Mainland or Free Zone operating company.

Does a trade licence guarantee business bank account approval?

No. Banks conduct their own due diligence, KYC and risk assessment. Account approval is always subject to the bank's decision.

How much does company formation cost?

Costs vary according to jurisdiction, activity, licence type, office requirements, visa allocation and additional approvals. A complete cost breakdown should be reviewed before making a decision.
