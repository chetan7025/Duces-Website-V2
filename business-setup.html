Important Considerations

Understand the Limitations of a Free Zone Company

A Free Zone company can be suitable for many businesses, but it is important to understand the operational and regulatory considerations before selecting this structure.

1. Mainland Market Access

Directly conducting certain activities in the UAE mainland may require the appropriate mainland licence, permits or arrangements. Requirements depend on the activity and applicable rules.

2. Activity Restrictions

A company can conduct only activities permitted under its licence. Some activities may require additional approvals from relevant authorities.

3. Visa Eligibility

Visa allocation is not automatically unlimited. It depends on the authority, package, facility and applicable immigration rules.

4. Banking Is Not Guaranteed

A Free Zone licence does not guarantee corporate bank account approval. Banks conduct their own compliance and risk assessment.

5. Additional Operating Costs

Establishment cards, visas, medical tests, Emirates ID, facilities, renewals and other services may involve additional charges.

6. Tax and Compliance

Free Zone status does not automatically mean zero corporate tax. Corporate Tax treatment depends on the applicable UAE tax rules and the company's circumstances.

Alternative Company Structure

Offshore Company Formation

An Offshore company may be considered for specific international holding, investment or asset-structuring purposes, depending on the applicable jurisdiction and legal requirements.

Key Considerations

  • Generally intended for international or holding structures.
  • Not designed as a standard UAE operating business licence.
  • Does not normally provide UAE residence visa eligibility.
  • Does not automatically provide the right to operate a UAE mainland business.
  • Bank account opening remains subject to bank approval and due diligence.

Offshore formation is not a replacement for a Mainland or Free Zone operating licence. Suitability should be assessed before proceeding.

UAE Mainland

Mainland Company Formation

Mainland company formation may be suitable for businesses that need to operate within the UAE local market, serve customers across different Emirates or participate in activities requiring a mainland licence.

  • Access to the UAE mainland market, subject to activity rules.
  • Suitable for many professional, commercial and service activities.
  • Office or premises requirements depend on the activity and authority.
  • Some activities require external approvals.
  • Ownership and legal structure depend on the activity and applicable regulations.
