01 / UAE Mainland

Mainland Company Formation

A UAE Mainland company may be suitable for businesses planning to serve customers within the UAE market, operate locally or carry out activities that require a mainland licence.

  • Access to UAE mainland markets, subject to activity rules.
  • Suitable for many commercial, professional and service activities.
  • Office and premises requirements depend on the activity.
  • Some activities require external authority approvals.
  • Ownership and legal structure depend on applicable regulations.

02 / UAE Free Zones

Free Zone Company Formation

UAE Free Zones provide company formation options through different licensing authorities. Each authority has its own approved activities, facilities, visa options and package structures.

  • 100% foreign ownership available under applicable structures.
  • Different authorities offer different business activity options.
  • Visa allocation depends on the authority and selected package.
  • Corporate bank account approval is subject to bank assessment.
  • Tax treatment depends on applicable UAE tax rules and eligibility.

03 / International Business Structure

Offshore Company Formation

An Offshore company may be considered for specific international holding, investment or asset-structuring purposes, subject to the applicable jurisdiction and legal requirements.

  • Generally intended for international or holding structures.
  • Not a standard UAE operating business licence.
  • Does not normally provide UAE residence visa eligibility.
  • Does not automatically permit UAE mainland business operations.
  • Banking remains subject to due diligence and bank approval.
