Offshore Company Setup

Offshore companies are generally considered for specific international business, holding or asset-structuring purposes, depending on the applicable jurisdiction and regulations.

Before You Apply

What Should You Consider Before Setting Up?

A company formation decision should account for the complete operating model, not only the initial licence price.

01. Business Activity

Identify the exact activities you intend to conduct and check whether additional approvals or conditions apply.

02. Ownership Structure

Review shareholders, legal form, ownership eligibility and management requirements.

03. Total Setup Cost

Consider licence fees, registration, facility, immigration, visa and renewal expenses.

04. Visa Requirements

Check the available visa allocation, eligibility, processing requirements and related costs.

05. Business Banking

Assess the proposed company's business model, expected transactions and bank documentation. Account opening remains subject to bank approval.

06. Tax & Compliance

Review applicable VAT, Corporate Tax, accounting and record-keeping obligations for the business.

Frequently Asked Questions

Business Setup FAQs

General answers to common questions about establishing a company in the UAE.

What is the difference between Mainland and Free Zone?

Mainland companies are licensed by the relevant Emirate authority. Free Zone companies are licensed by a specific Free Zone authority. Their activities, operating permissions, facilities and visa arrangements differ according to the applicable rules.

Can a foreign national own a UAE company?

Foreign ownership is permitted for many UAE business activities, but the applicable ownership rules depend on the activity, legal structure and relevant authority.

Does every company need a physical office?

Requirements vary. Some authorities offer flexi-desk or shared facility arrangements, while other activities or structures may require dedicated premises.

Can a new company open a UAE business bank account?

A newly established company may apply for a business bank account. Each bank conducts its own eligibility, KYC and compliance assessment. Account opening is subject to the bank's approval.

Does a company need to register for VAT and Corporate Tax?

Registration depends on the applicable UAE tax rules, thresholds, business circumstances and exemptions. Each company should assess its obligations separately.

How much does UAE company formation cost?

Costs depend on the authority, activity, legal form, facility, visa allocation and additional approvals. A suitable quotation should identify the inclusions and recurring costs.
