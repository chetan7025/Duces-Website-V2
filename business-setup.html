UAE Business Setup & Company Formation

Explore the right company structure for your business. We guide you through mainland, free zone and offshore company formation in the UAE.

Mainland Company Formation

A mainland company can be suitable for businesses planning to operate across the UAE market, work with local customers and explore government or private-sector opportunities.

Key considerations

  • Access to the UAE mainland market, subject to the licensed activity and applicable rules.
  • Many business activities permit 100% foreign ownership.
  • Choice of commercial, professional and other permitted activities.
  • Office and premises requirements depend on the activity and licensing authority.
  • Additional external approvals may apply to certain regulated activities.

DUCES helps you understand the available business structures, activity requirements, documentation and licensing process.

Free Zone Company Formation

UAE free zones offer different licensing options for entrepreneurs, consultants, traders, service providers and international businesses.

Key considerations

  • Free zone options for different industries and business activities.
  • 100% foreign ownership under applicable free zone structures.
  • Different licence, shareholder and visa package options.
  • Flexible workspace solutions offered by selected authorities.
  • Business activity and market access rules vary by jurisdiction.

We help compare suitable free zones based on your activity, operational requirements, budget and visa needs.

Offshore Company Formation

Offshore companies are generally considered for specific international business, holding and investment structures. They are not the same as a mainland or free zone operating company.

Key considerations

  • May be suitable for certain international holding or investment structures.
  • Can be considered for international ownership arrangements.
  • Usually does not provide the same UAE operational permissions as a mainland or free zone licence.
  • Banking, compliance, documentation and substance requirements need careful review.
  • Tax treatment depends on the company's activities, structure and applicable laws.

Is an offshore company right for you?

The correct structure depends on what you intend to do, where your customers are located and how the company will operate. Speak with our team before deciding.

Company Formation – Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about starting and registering a business in the UAE.

1. How do I start a company in the UAE?

The usual process starts with understanding your business activity, selecting a suitable jurisdiction, choosing a legal structure, reserving a trade name, preparing the required documents and applying for the business licence. Additional approvals, premises, immigration and visa steps may apply depending on the business.

2. What is the difference between mainland, free zone and offshore companies?

Mainland companies are licensed for permitted business activities in the UAE mainland market. Free zone companies are registered under a specific free zone authority and follow its licensing and operating rules. Offshore companies are generally used for specific international or holding structures and do not carry the same operating permissions as a regular UAE business licence.

3. Can a foreign national own 100% of a UAE company?

Many UAE mainland activities and free zone structures allow 100% foreign ownership. Certain strategic or regulated activities may have additional conditions. Ownership should be checked against the exact activity and relevant authority before applying.

4. What documents are normally required for company formation?

Common documents include passport copies of shareholders and managers, contact details, residential address information and proposed business activities. Depending on the authority, legal structure and activity, additional documents may be requested.

5. How much does it cost to set up a company in the UAE?

The cost depends on jurisdiction, business activity, licence type, number of shareholders, visa requirements, office or workspace requirements and government charges. Request a written quotation separating licence fees from visa, immigration, medical, Emirates ID, office and renewal costs.

6. How long does UAE company formation take?

Processing time varies by authority, activity, document readiness and external approvals. A confirmed timeline can only be provided after reviewing the proposed setup and the authority's current process.

7. Do I need an office to register a company in the UAE?

It depends on the jurisdiction and licence. Some free zones provide flexi-desk or shared workspace options, while other activities or structures require dedicated premises. Mainland requirements also depend on the activity and licensing authority.

8. Can I get a UAE residence visa with my company?

Certain company licences and packages may allow eligible shareholders or employees to apply for residence visas. The number of visas depends on the authority, package, workspace and applicable immigration requirements. Visa issuance remains subject to government approval.

9. Does a company licence guarantee a business bank account?

No. A trade licence does not guarantee bank account approval. Banks independently review company activities, ownership, business model, source of funds, expected transactions and supporting documents. DUCES can assist with preparation and application coordination, but the bank makes the final decision.

10. Can I change my business activity or add a new activity later?

In many cases, a company can apply to add, remove or amend business activities. Approval depends on the licensing authority, proposed activity and any additional requirements. Fees and supporting documents may apply.

11. Do UAE companies need VAT and Corporate Tax registration?

VAT and Corporate Tax obligations depend on the company's activities, taxable supplies, revenue, status and applicable UAE tax rules. Free zone registration does not automatically mean every income stream is tax-free. Businesses should assess their registration, filing and record-keeping obligations with qualified tax support.

12. Can DUCES help me choose the right jurisdiction?

Yes. DUCES can discuss your proposed activity, ownership, expected customers, visa needs, workspace requirements and budget, then help you compare suitable mainland, free zone and offshore options. Final eligibility and approval remain with the relevant authorities.
