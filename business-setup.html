Business Setup in the UAE

A Clear Route from Business Idea to Company Formation

The UAE offers different company formation structures for entrepreneurs, investors, consultants and established businesses. The appropriate route depends on your business activity, target customers, ownership requirements, operational plans and budget.

Mainland, Free Zone and Offshore structures serve different purposes. Selecting a structure only on the basis of its advertised starting price may lead to additional costs or operational limitations later.

Our approach is to understand your business requirements first, then help you evaluate suitable setup options.

What We Help You Evaluate

  • Business activity and licence category
  • Mainland or Free Zone jurisdiction
  • Ownership and shareholder structure
  • Office and workspace requirements
  • Visa eligibility and requirements
  • Estimated formation and renewal costs
  • Post-formation business requirements
Choose Your Business Structure

Three Main Routes for UAE Company Formation

Each structure has different licensing rules, operating conditions and cost considerations. Review the options before deciding.

01 / MAINLAND

Mainland Company Setup

A mainland licence may suit businesses planning to operate within the UAE market, work with local clients or undertake activities requiring mainland licensing.

  • Licence based on approved business activities
  • Suitable for many UAE-facing operations
  • Office and regulatory requirements vary

02 / FREE ZONE

Free Zone Company Setup

Free Zones offer different licensing packages and business structures. The right authority depends on your activity, facilities, visa needs and operating model.

  • Different authorities and licence packages
  • Options for office, flexi-desk or other facilities
  • Visa allocation depends on the package and rules

03 / OFFSHORE

Offshore Company Setup

Offshore structures may be considered for specific holding, investment or international business purposes, subject to the relevant authority's regulations.

  • Designed for permitted offshore purposes
  • Not a substitute for an operating UAE licence
  • Banking and compliance checks still apply

Understand the Difference

Mainland vs Free Zone vs Offshore

This is a general overview. Actual permissions, eligibility and requirements depend on the activity, authority and applicable rules.

Consideration Mainland Free Zone Offshore
Primary purpose Operating business in the UAE market Operating within the relevant Free Zone framework Permitted holding or international structures
Licence Mainland commercial or professional licence, as applicable Licence issued by the selected Free Zone authority Offshore incorporation or registration
Office requirements Depend on activity and licensing authority Depend on authority and selected package Physical operating office is generally not the purpose
Visa options Subject to establishment and applicable requirements Subject to package, facility and authority rules Generally not designed for UAE residence visas
UAE market operations Generally suitable, subject to activity permissions Subject to applicable laws and activity-specific rules Not intended as a regular UAE operating licence
Best evaluated for UAE-facing commercial operations Businesses matching a Free Zone's permitted activities Specific holding and international structuring needs

This table is an introductory guide, not a legal or regulatory determination. Confirm the latest rules with the relevant authority before incorporation.

Prepare in Advance

Common Documents for Company Formation

Documentation varies according to the jurisdiction, activity, shareholders and legal structure. The relevant authority may request additional information.

A document checklist will be confirmed after reviewing your proposed activity and selected jurisdiction. Do not submit original documents unless the relevant authority requires them.
Passport Copy For shareholders, directors and authorised signatories, as applicable.
Photograph Recent passport-size photograph where required.
Contact Details Current email address, mobile number and residential details.
Business Information Proposed business activity, company name options and operational details.
Additional Documents Existing UAE visa, Emirates ID, NOC or other documents where applicable.
