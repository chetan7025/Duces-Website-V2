The Right Structure Makes a Difference

The UAE offers several company formation routes for entrepreneurs, investors, consultants and established businesses. Each route has different licensing conditions, permitted activities, facilities and compliance requirements.

The best option is not always the cheapest advertised package. Your business activity, customer base, ownership, visa needs, office requirements and future plans should guide the decision.

DUCES helps you assess these factors before proceeding with the relevant licensing authority.

01 — Mainland Company Formation

UAE Mainland Business Setup

Mainland company formation may be appropriate for businesses intending to serve customers across the UAE market, undertake approved commercial or professional activities, or operate from a suitable mainland location.

Licensing and ownership requirements depend on the selected activity, legal form and relevant emirate authority.

Enquire About Mainland Setup

Mainland Setup Considerations

  • Selection of approved business activities
  • Legal form and shareholder arrangements
  • Trade name reservation and initial approval
  • Office, tenancy and Ejari requirements where applicable
  • External approvals for regulated activities where required
  • Licence issuance and establishment procedures
  • Visa and immigration requirements

03 — Offshore Company Formation

UAE Offshore Company Setup

Offshore incorporation is a specialised option that may be considered for permitted international business, asset holding or investment structuring purposes.

It should not be confused with a Mainland or Free Zone operating licence. Offshore companies generally do not provide the same operating permissions or UAE residence visa route as an operating company.

Enquire About Offshore Setup

Important Considerations

  • Purpose and proposed activities
  • Eligible jurisdiction and incorporation rules
  • Shareholder and beneficial ownership information
  • Registered agent and registered office requirements
  • Banking eligibility and due diligence
  • Ongoing compliance and reporting obligations

Mainland vs Free Zone vs Offshore

A general comparison to help you understand the purpose of each structure.

Consideration Mainland Free Zone Offshore
Primary purpose UAE operating business Business under Free Zone framework Permitted holding or international purposes
Licence Mainland trade or professional licence Licence from selected authority Offshore incorporation
Office requirements Depend on activity and authority Depend on authority and package Registered office requirements apply
Visa options Subject to applicable establishment rules Subject to package and authority rules Generally not a UAE residence visa route
UAE market operations Subject to approved activities Subject to applicable laws and permissions Not a regular UAE operating licence
Suitable for UAE-facing businesses Businesses matching permitted Free Zone activities Specific international structures

This is a general guide, not a legal determination. Actual permissions, tax treatment, ownership conditions and operating rules depend on the structure, authority and applicable UAE regulations.
