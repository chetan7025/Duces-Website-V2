International Corporate Structures

Offshore Company Formation

Offshore structures may be considered for certain international holding, investment and corporate purposes. They are different from operating Mainland and Free Zone companies and have their own regulatory requirements.

Important Considerations

  • Purpose and intended use of the structure
  • Applicable jurisdiction and eligibility
  • Shareholder and beneficial ownership documents
  • Registered office and agent requirements
  • Banking, tax and compliance implications
  • Restrictions on local commercial operations

Offshore formation is not suitable for every business. We help you understand the broad considerations and coordinate with relevant specialists where required.

Common Questions

Business Setup FAQs

Which is better: Mainland or Free Zone?

Neither is universally better. The right choice depends on your business activity, target customers, office needs, visa requirements, budget and applicable licensing rules.

Can a foreign investor own 100% of a UAE company?

Many UAE business activities and structures permit full foreign ownership, subject to the applicable activity, jurisdiction and regulatory requirements. Confirm the specific case before proceeding.

Does a Free Zone company receive a UAE trade licence?

Yes. The selected Free Zone authority issues the relevant licence for approved activities, subject to its licensing process and requirements.

Can a Free Zone company operate in the UAE Mainland?

Mainland market access depends on the activity, applicable regulations and required arrangements or approvals. It should be assessed before choosing a structure.

How long does company formation take?

Timelines vary by authority, activity, document readiness, external approvals and application requirements. A confirmed timeline should be obtained for the selected route.

Can DUCES guarantee licence or visa approval?

No. Final approval and issuance decisions rest with the relevant licensing, immigration and government authorities. DUCES provides consultancy and coordination support.
