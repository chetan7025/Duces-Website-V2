Frequently Asked Questions

Auditing & Taxation – FAQs

Does every UAE company need VAT registration?

No. VAT registration depends on applicable taxable supply thresholds and other conditions under UAE VAT legislation. Businesses should review their individual circumstances.

Do Free Zone companies have to consider Corporate Tax?

Yes. Free Zone companies are within the UAE Corporate Tax framework. A 0% rate may apply to qualifying income where all relevant conditions are met. It is not automatic for every Free Zone company.

Can DUCES help with bookkeeping?

DUCES can discuss bookkeeping and accounting support requirements and coordinate suitable services based on the business's needs and the qualifications required.

Does DUCES issue independent audit reports?

Independent audit opinions must be issued by appropriately qualified and authorised audit professionals. DUCES can assist with document organisation and coordination.

Can DUCES help with Corporate Tax returns?

DUCES can assist with general documentation and coordinate return-related work with qualified professionals or appropriately authorised tax service providers where required.

What documents should I prepare?

Common documents include the trade licence, company formation documents, invoices, bank statements, expense records and financial information. Exact requirements depend on the service.
