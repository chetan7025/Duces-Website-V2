About DUCES

Business consultancy and coordination support designed to help entrepreneurs and companies navigate their business requirements in the United Arab Emirates.

Who We Are

DUCES Consultancy Services is a UAE-focused business consultancy dedicated to helping entrepreneurs, investors and established businesses understand and manage their business requirements.

From company formation and business banking to business finance, taxation support and PRO services, we aim to make complex business processes easier to understand and coordinate.

We believe every business requirement deserves clear communication, practical guidance and a structured approach.

Our Purpose

To simplify business-related processes by connecting clients with suitable solutions, explaining requirements clearly and coordinating the necessary steps through the relevant service providers and authorities.
