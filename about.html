About DUCES

Practical Business Support. Clearer Decisions. Better Direction.

DUCES Consultancy Services supports entrepreneurs, business owners and companies navigating business establishment and essential business services in the UAE.

Who We Are

One Consultancy for Your Business Journey

Starting and managing a business involves more than obtaining a trade licence. Business owners also need to consider banking, financial requirements, taxation, documentation and ongoing administrative responsibilities.

DUCES brings these service areas together through a structured consultancy approach. We help clients understand available options, prepare for requirements and coordinate the next steps with relevant service providers and authorities.

Our Purpose

To make business-related decisions easier to understand by providing clear information, practical guidance and coordinated support throughout the business journey.

Our Service Model

Independent Consultancy. Clear Service Scope.

DUCES Consultancy Services operates as an independent consultancy. We provide guidance, documentation assistance and coordination within our applicable service scope.

We are not a bank, financial institution, government authority, audit firm or regulator. Banking, lending, licensing, tax and immigration decisions remain with the relevant institutions and authorities. Where a service requires specific professional qualifications or authorisation, it must be handled by an appropriately qualified or authorised provider.
