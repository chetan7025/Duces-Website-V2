Who we are

About DUCES Consultancy Services

DUCES Consultancy Services is a business consultancy focused on helping entrepreneurs and businesses navigate selected business setup, banking support, documentation and business service requirements.

We believe that good support begins with listening carefully, explaining options clearly and setting realistic expectations about processes, documents and timelines.

Our Purpose

To make business processes easier to understand and help clients take informed, organised steps toward their business goals.
