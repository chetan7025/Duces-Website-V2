FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

PRO & Visa FAQs

1. What is a UAE establishment card?

It is a company-related registration document used in relevant immigration processes. Requirements and procedures depend on the company's licensing and immigration authority.

2. Can a company owner apply for an investor or partner visa?

Eligible company owners and partners may apply through the applicable route. Approval depends on company documents, immigration conditions and the relevant authority.

3. What is involved in an employment visa process?

Depending on the employer's jurisdiction and the applicant, the process may involve work permit approval, entry permit or status change, medical fitness, Emirates ID and residence formalities.

4. Can a UAE resident sponsor family members?

Eligible residents may sponsor qualifying family members subject to current sponsorship, income, accommodation and documentation requirements.

5. Is medical fitness required for residence visas?

Medical fitness screening is required for many residence visa categories, subject to the applicant's age, visa type and applicable regulations.

6. Can DUCES guarantee visa approval?

No. DUCES provides documentation and application coordination. Visa approval and issuance are determined by the relevant UAE government authorities.

7. Can DUCES assist with visa renewal and cancellation?

Yes. We can help organise the relevant documentation and coordinate applications, subject to the applicant's circumstances and applicable authority procedures.
