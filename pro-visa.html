Frequently Asked Questions

PRO & Visa – Common Questions

Can DUCES help with employment visas?

DUCES can assist with general documentation guidance and coordination of relevant employment visa procedures. Eligibility and approval depend on the employer, applicant and applicable authorities.

Can a company owner apply for an investor visa?

Eligible company owners or partners may qualify for an appropriate residency route, subject to the licensing authority and immigration requirements.

Can DUCES help with family sponsorship?

DUCES can help explain general sponsorship documentation and coordinate the relevant application process. The sponsor must meet the applicable conditions.

Does DUCES guarantee visa approval?

No. All visa, work permit, Emirates ID and residency decisions are subject to the relevant authority's eligibility rules and approval process.

Can DUCES assist with document attestation?

We can help identify general documentation requirements and coordinate with appropriate service providers where attestation or translation is required.

What documents should I provide initially?

The initial checklist depends on the service. Passport, existing visa or Emirates ID details, company information and relevant supporting documents may be requested.
